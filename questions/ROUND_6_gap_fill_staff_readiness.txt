=====================================================================
ROUND 6 - GAP FILL & STAFF-LEVEL READINESS
"What the other five rounds don't cover, but a Staff / Architect-track
 Backend + DevOps + Cloud + AI interview will."
Duration: 50 min assessment + 10 min general
=====================================================================

WHY THIS ROUND EXISTS
Rounds 1-5 and DSA_SQL are heavily anchored on his resume. That's right for
defending claims, but interviews at the next level also probe things he hasn't
listed and may not have used. This bank deliberately avoids repeating questions
already in Rounds 1-5 and covers the gaps:
  A. Staff-level leadership & influence      (Round 1 behavioural is resume-bound)
  B. Generic behavioural / STAR stories       (asked in almost every loop)
  C. Distributed systems theory               (consensus, quorums, consistency models)
  D. Architecture patterns & API styles       (DDD, CQRS, event sourcing, GraphQL)
  E. Kubernetes & platform depth              (Round 2 only covers basics)
  F. Linux, networking & TLS depth
  G. Security engineering depth
  H. Data engineering & event streaming
  I. Performance, capacity & estimation drills
  J. Modern Python & code reading
  K. AI infrastructure & newer AI engineering (GPUs, fine-tuning, agent frameworks)
  L. System designs not in Round 4

HOW TO RUN THIS ROUND
- Unlike other rounds, this is a ROTATING bank. Pick one or two sections per session
  based on knowledge_map.json gaps, ~8-12 questions total.
- Sections A and B: ask for a concrete story every time. "I would..." answers without
  "I did..." are a flag at staff level. Push for scope, impact numbers, and what he
  personally drove.
- Sections C, E, F, I: rapid-fire is fine (60-120s each). Grade on precision, not recall
  of trivia - "I don't know, here's how I'd reason about it" is acceptable.
- Section J has code to read aloud. In voice mode, read the snippet slowly and ask
  "what's wrong with this?" before giving any hint.
- Section L designs follow the Round 4 rubric (clarify, numbers, API/data, architecture,
  deep dives, failure modes, trade-offs). Pick at most one per session.

RED FLAGS TO WATCH FOR
- Staff questions answered purely as an individual contributor ("I wrote the code").
- Theory answered with buzzwords and no mechanism (e.g. "Raft is for consensus" and stops).
- Security answered as a checklist with no threat model.
- AI infra answered only from the API-consumer side, with no idea what happens on a GPU.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
A. STAFF-LEVEL LEADERSHIP & INFLUENCE
---------------------------------------------------------------------
1.  In your own words, what is the difference between a senior engineer and a staff
    engineer? Give an example of staff-level work you have done, or would do in your
    first six months here.
2.  You believe the team should move from X to Y (e.g. Celery to a workflow engine).
    Walk me through getting buy-in from engineers, your manager and product, without
    formal authority.
3.  How do you run an RFC / design-review process so it is neither a bottleneck nor a
    rubber stamp? What is an ADR, and when do you write one instead of an RFC?
4.  You are asked to estimate a 6-month migration. How do you estimate, communicate
    uncertainty, and re-plan when you are 40% over budget at month 2?
5.  How do you decide which technical debt to pay down and which to live with? Make the
    case to a product manager in business terms.
6.  Two senior engineers strongly disagree on an architecture choice and it is blocking
    the team. What do you do?
7.  How do you grow a mid-level engineer into a senior? What do you actually do week to
    week, and how do you know it is working?
8.  Conway's law: how has team structure shaped a system you worked on? How would you
    use the "inverse Conway manoeuvre" deliberately?
9.  What are good engineering metrics? What do the DORA metrics measure, and how can they
    be gamed or misused?
10. How do you set technical direction for a team for the next 12 months? What goes into
    a technical strategy document, and how do you keep it from going stale?

---------------------------------------------------------------------
B. GENERIC BEHAVIOURAL (STAR - situation, task, action, result)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
11. Tell me about a time you influenced a decision outside your own team.
12. Tell me about a time you had to deliver bad news to a stakeholder or client (a missed
    deadline, a failed approach, a cost overrun).
13. Tell me about a time you made a high-stakes decision with incomplete information.
14. Tell me about a time you received critical feedback. What did you change?
15. Tell me about a time you said no to a senior stakeholder or client, and what happened.
16. Tell me about a time you led work in a technology where you were not the expert.
17. Tell me about a time you improved a process (not code) that made the whole team faster.
18. Tell me about a time you traded quality for speed under deadline pressure. How did it
    turn out, and would you make the same call again?

---------------------------------------------------------------------
C. DISTRIBUTED SYSTEMS THEORY
---------------------------------------------------------------------
19. Linearizability vs serializability vs sequential consistency vs eventual consistency:
    explain each with an example. What does "strict serializable" mean?
20. Explain Raft at a high level: terms, leader election, log replication. Why do you need
    a majority quorum, and why are clusters usually 3 or 5 nodes?
21. Quorum reads and writes (R + W > N): explain them. What anomalies can still happen even
    when the inequality holds?
22. Two-phase commit: how does it work, why does it block, and why do microservice
    architectures avoid it?
23. What is split-brain? How do leases and fencing tokens prevent it?
24. What are CRDTs? Explain one (G-Counter, LWW-Register or OR-Set) and where you would use it.
25. Tail latency at scale: why does a fan-out to 100 backends make p99 latency the common
    case? Explain hedged requests and tied requests.
26. Design the timeout budget for a request that calls three services in sequence with a
    2-second SLA. What is deadline propagation, and what goes wrong without it?
27. Gossip protocols and failure detection (e.g. phi-accrual): where are they used, and why
    not just send heartbeats to a central server?
28. What is a metastable failure? Why can a system stay down after the original trigger
    is gone, and how do you design against it?

---------------------------------------------------------------------
D. ARCHITECTURE PATTERNS & API STYLES
---------------------------------------------------------------------
29. Domain-Driven Design: bounded context, aggregate, entity vs value object, ubiquitous
    language. Map Aurora/Pulse (quote, policy, rater, broker) into bounded contexts.
30. What is an aggregate's consistency boundary, and why should one transaction modify
    only one aggregate? What do you do when a business rule spans two?
31. CQRS: when is it worth it and when is it overkill? How does it change what the UI has
    to deal with?
32. Event sourcing: how does it work, what are snapshots, how do you evolve event schemas
    (upcasting), and when would you refuse to use it?
33. Commands vs domain events vs integration events: what is the difference, and how do you
    name and version each?
34. GraphQL: when would you pick it over REST? How do you deal with the N+1 problem
    (DataLoader), caching, authorization per field, and query-cost limits?
35. API gateway vs backend-for-frontend (BFF) vs service mesh: what does each own?
36. How do you deprecate and remove a public API endpoint that external partners depend on?
    Timeline, usage signals, communication, and the final switch-off.
37. Synchronous vs asynchronous communication between services: decision criteria. What is
    temporal coupling, and how do you spot it in an architecture diagram?
38. Workflow engines (Temporal, Azure Durable Functions, Step Functions) vs a hand-rolled
    state machine with a queue: when is the engine worth it? How does Temporal achieve
    durable execution, and why must workflow code be deterministic?

---------------------------------------------------------------------
E. KUBERNETES & PLATFORM DEPTH
---------------------------------------------------------------------
39. What happens, component by component, when you run `kubectl apply` on a Deployment?
    (API server, etcd, controllers, scheduler, kubelet, container runtime, kube-proxy.)
40. Scheduling controls: node affinity, pod anti-affinity, taints and tolerations, topology
    spread constraints. Design the placement of a highly available service across 3 zones.
41. PodDisruptionBudgets: what do they protect against, and how can a badly configured PDB
    block a cluster upgrade or node drain?
42. Service types (ClusterIP, NodePort, LoadBalancer, headless): how does traffic actually
    reach a pod via kube-proxy (iptables/IPVS) or an eBPF data plane like Cilium?
43. Helm vs Kustomize: when do you use each? What are the pain points of Helm at scale?
44. GitOps with Argo CD or Flux: how does reconciliation work, how are secrets handled, and
    how do you promote a release between environments?
45. Operators and CRDs: what is the operator pattern, and when would you write an operator
    instead of shipping a Helm chart?
46. Service mesh (Istio, Linkerd): which problems does it solve (mTLS, retries, traffic
    splitting, telemetry), what does it cost, and when is it not worth it?
47. Kubernetes RBAC: Role vs ClusterRole, ServiceAccounts, and how a pod gets a cloud
    identity (AKS workload identity / EKS IRSA) without any stored secret.
48. How do you upgrade a production AKS/EKS cluster with zero downtime? Control plane vs node
    pools, surge settings, version skew, and deprecated API removals.
49. Admission control: Pod Security Standards, OPA Gatekeeper, Kyverno. Which policies would
    you enforce on day one, and how do you roll them out without breaking existing workloads?
50. CoreDNS and `ndots:5`: why can DNS lookups from pods be slow or flaky, and how do you fix it?

---------------------------------------------------------------------
F. LINUX, NETWORKING & TLS DEPTH
---------------------------------------------------------------------
51. Walk through a TLS 1.3 handshake. What is SNI, how is the certificate chain validated,
    and what changes with mutual TLS?
52. A service intermittently fails with "connection reset by peer". List your hypotheses in
    order (idle timeouts, keep-alive mismatch, conntrack table, load balancer behaviour...).
53. Ephemeral port exhaustion and TIME_WAIT: when does a high-throughput client hit them,
    and how do you fix it?
54. Load-balancing algorithms: round robin, least connections, consistent hashing,
    power-of-two-choices. When does each one behave badly?
55. HTTP keep-alive and HTTP/2 multiplexing: why does gRPC load balancing break behind an L4
    load balancer, and how do you fix it?
56. strace, lsof, ss, tcpdump, perf: what does each tell you? Give a real debugging
    situation for each.
57. The Linux OOM killer: how does it pick a victim, and how does cgroup v2 memory
    accounting relate to Kubernetes memory limits?
58. What does a CDN actually do? Cache keys, TTL vs stale-while-revalidate, purging, and
    cases where putting a CDN in front makes things worse.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
G. SECURITY ENGINEERING DEPTH
---------------------------------------------------------------------
59. Threat-model this feature with STRIDE: a broker portal where brokers upload documents
    and receive an AI-generated summary.
60. OWASP API Security Top 10: explain BOLA (broken object level authorization). How do you
    prevent it systematically in a FastAPI codebase, not endpoint by endpoint?
61. Envelope encryption with KMS / Key Vault: how does it work, why not encrypt data directly
    with the master key, and how does key rotation work without re-encrypting everything?
62. "Zero trust": what does it mean concretely for service-to-service calls inside a cluster?
63. XSS, CSRF and clickjacking: which ones matter for a JSON API using cookie auth vs bearer
    tokens? What do SameSite cookie settings change?
64. Container hardening: minimal/distroless base images, read-only root filesystem, dropped
    Linux capabilities, seccomp, non-root. What is your baseline for every service?
65. JWT validation mistakes: algorithm confusion (`alg: none`, RS256 vs HS256), missing
    `aud`/`iss` checks, clock skew, JWKS caching and key rotation. How do you validate tokens
    correctly in a service?
66. A penetration test reports 30 findings one week before launch. How do you triage, what
    do you fix first, and what do you formally accept as risk?

---------------------------------------------------------------------
H. DATA ENGINEERING & EVENT STREAMING
---------------------------------------------------------------------
67. Batch vs streaming vs micro-batch: decision criteria. What are the Lambda and Kappa
    architectures?
68. Lakehouse basics: Parquet, Delta Lake, Apache Iceberg. What do table formats add over
    plain Parquet files in S3/Blob (ACID, time travel, schema evolution)?
69. Kafka log compaction, retention and tombstones: when do you use a compacted topic?
70. Event schema evolution: Avro / Protobuf / JSON Schema with a schema registry. Explain
    backward, forward and full compatibility, and which side (producer or consumer) needs which.
71. Data contracts: what are they, and how do they stop an upstream team from silently
    breaking your pipeline?
72. Airflow / Dagster: DAGs, idempotent tasks, backfills, and why tasks should not pass large
    data between each other through the orchestrator (e.g. Airflow XCom).
73. Stream processing: event time vs processing time, watermarks, tumbling/sliding/session
    windows, late data. Explain with "gross written premium per hour".
74. Row vs columnar storage: why columnar compresses well and scans fast, and at what point
    Postgres stops being enough for analytics.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
I. PERFORMANCE, CAPACITY & ESTIMATION DRILLS
---------------------------------------------------------------------
75. State Little's Law and use it: a service handles 500 RPS at 200ms average latency. How
    many requests are in flight? How many uvicorn workers or threads do you need?
76. Estimation drill: storage and monthly growth for 10 years of policy documents. 2M policies,
    3 documents each at issue (avg 400KB), plus 2 endorsement documents per policy per year.
    Then: cost in hot vs cool vs archive storage.
77. Latency numbers every engineer should know: L1/L2 cache, RAM, SSD read, same-zone
    network round trip, cross-region round trip. Rough orders of magnitude, and why they
    matter in design.
78. Amdahl's Law and the Universal Scalability Law: why doesn't adding replicas scale
    throughput linearly?
79. How do you design a load test that reflects production? Open vs closed workload models,
    coordinated omission, ramp-up, realistic data and think time.
80. Queueing intuition: why does latency explode as utilisation approaches 100%? What
    utilisation do you target for a latency-sensitive service, and why?
81. Capacity planning for next year: what data do you collect, how do you forecast, and how
    much headroom do you keep?
82. A performance regression appeared somewhere in the last 50 commits. How do you find it?
    (git bisect, benchmark suites in CI, continuous profiling.)

---------------------------------------------------------------------
J. MODERN PYTHON & CODE READING
---------------------------------------------------------------------
83. Python packaging in 2026: pyproject.toml, wheels vs sdists, lockfiles, uv vs pip vs
    Poetry. How do you guarantee reproducible builds in a Docker image?
84. Typing beyond basics: TypeVar, Generic, ParamSpec (for typing decorators), Literal,
    @overload, TypeIs/TypeGuard, Self. Where have you needed one in real code?
85. Structural pattern matching (`match`/`case`): give a good use case and a misuse.
86. What is wrong with this code?
        async def fetch_all(urls):
            tasks = []
            for url in urls:
                tasks.append(asyncio.create_task(fetch(url)))
            return [t.result() for t in tasks]
87. Read each snippet aloud. What is the bug?
    (a) handlers = [lambda: i for i in range(3)]; print([h() for h in handlers])
    (b) for key in config:
            if config[key] is None: del config[key]
    (c) premium = 0.1 + 0.2   # later: if premium == 0.3: apply_discount()
    (d) session = Session()   # module level, shared by every request in a threaded server
    (e) while True:
            try: return call_api()
            except Exception: pass
88. Dates and timezones: naive vs aware datetimes, why `datetime.utcnow()` is deprecated,
    DST transitions, and how you store and compare policy effective dates that start at
    midnight local time in different countries.
89. Money in Python: why float is wrong, `Decimal` contexts, rounding modes (ROUND_HALF_UP
    vs ROUND_HALF_EVEN), `quantize`, and how this connects to matching Excel's ROUND.
90. pandas vs Polars vs DuckDB: memory model, performance, and when you would reach for each
    inside a backend service or a data job.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
K. AI INFRASTRUCTURE & NEWER AI ENGINEERING
---------------------------------------------------------------------
91. Serving open models on Kubernetes: GPU node pools, the NVIDIA device plugin, MIG vs
    time-slicing, model load times, and autoscaling GPU workloads (scale-to-zero trade-offs).
92. LoRA / QLoRA fine-tuning: what is actually trained, what memory it needs, and how you
    serve many LoRA adapters on one base model.
93. Distillation: using a large model to label data and train a small model for a narrow task
    (e.g. classifying submission emails). Walk through the workflow and how you decide it is
    worth it.
94. Context engineering for long-running agents: what goes into the context window over a long
    task - memory, compaction/summarisation, scratchpads, tool-result retrieval. How do you keep
    the agent coherent after 200 steps?
95. Agent frameworks and protocols: LangGraph, OpenAI Agents SDK, Claude Agent SDK, Semantic
    Kernel / AutoGen, Pydantic AI; MCP vs A2A. What does each solve, and how do you choose?
96. Vision-language models for documents: when do you send page images to a multimodal model
    instead of running OCR and sending text? Cost, accuracy, and failure modes.
97. Embedding 50M chunks: throughput under rate limits, cost estimate, idempotent resumable
    jobs, and storage size (dimensions x float32 vs quantised or binary vectors).
98. Real-time voice agent (speech-to-text -> LLM -> text-to-speech): build the latency budget.
    Where does the time go, and how do you get the response under ~800ms?
99. AI security beyond prompt injection: RAG corpus poisoning, model and tool supply chain
    (malicious MCP servers, pickle-based model files), excessive agency. Walk through the
    OWASP Top 10 for LLM applications.
100. Responsible AI and regulation: EU AI Act risk tiers, why some insurance and hiring uses
     count as "high-risk", and which engineering artefacts (logging, documentation, human
     oversight, accuracy monitoring) you would build to comply.
