=====================================================================
ROUND 2 - FUNDAMENTALS ACROSS HIS DECLARED STACK
"Does he actually know the tools he listed, or has he only used them?"
Duration: 50 min assessment + 10 min general
=====================================================================

HOW TO RUN THIS ROUND
- This is a BREADTH round. Rapid fire. 30-90 seconds per answer.
- Every question below maps to something he explicitly listed on the resume.
- Scoring suggestion: pick 3 questions from each section. If he stalls on the first
  two of a section, move on - that section is a "used it, doesn't know it".
- Tell him upfront: "I'd rather you say 'I don't know' than guess."
- Calibration signal: he lists 40+ technologies. Expect deep on Python/FastAPI/
  Postgres/Redis/Docker/K8s/Celery. Shallow on Kafka internals, Terraform state,
  Memcached, Athena is acceptable - but he should KNOW he's shallow there.
