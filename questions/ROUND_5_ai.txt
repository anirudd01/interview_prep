CONTEXT ON THE CANDIDATE
Resume AI claims: Ollama, RAG, pgVector, ChromaDB, Groq; a RAG pipeline over org
documents; an MCP server for a QA team; an "agent" replacing the Cellular Excel->Python
transpiler; information extraction from insurance agent inboxes (Orbit/Ayana); Azure AI
Search indexers; a resume-to-JD matching system (Jio ATS); and an RLHF evaluation
platform (Turing).

Calibration: he is an AI-adjacent BACKEND engineer, not an ML researcher. Do NOT
grade him on transformer math or training theory. DO grade him hard on:
  - RAG quality engineering (chunking, retrieval, evaluation) - not just "I used pgVector"
  - Evaluation. If he has no eval methodology, that is the single biggest red flag.
  - Cost, latency, and failure handling of LLM systems in production
  - Agent/tool-calling design and its safety and determinism problems
  - Knowing where NOT to use an LLM
Sections A-D are the core. E-G are for depth if he's strong. H is the pass/fail section.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
A. FOUNDATIONS - ENOUGH TO BE DANGEROUS (rapid, 30-60s each)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
1.  What is a token? Why does token count matter for cost, latency AND accuracy?
2.  Explain embeddings to a backend engineer. What does cosine similarity actually
    measure, and when is it misleading?
3.  What is the context window, and what actually happens when you exceed it?
4.  Temperature, top_p, top_k, seed - what does each do and what do you set for a
    data-extraction task vs a summarization task?
5.  Why are LLM outputs non-deterministic even at temperature 0?
6.  What is the "lost in the middle" problem in long contexts?
7.  Base model vs instruct model vs reasoning model - when does each make sense?
8.  What is a system prompt, and why does prompt order matter?
9.  Explain prompt injection. Now explain indirect prompt injection via a retrieved
    document. Which one applies to your Orbit/Ayana email pipeline?
10. What is structured output / JSON mode / constrained decoding? How do you guarantee
    a schema? What do you do when the model still returns invalid JSON?
11. What is quantization (Q4, Q8, GGUF) and what do you lose? (Ties to his Ollama use.)
12. What is a context-window KV cache and why does prompt caching cut cost so much?
13. When is an LLM the WRONG tool? Give me three tasks from your own projects where a
    regex, a classifier, or plain code beats an LLM.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
B. RAG - DESIGN AND QUALITY (his strongest claim; go deep)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
14. Draw your org-knowledge RAG pipeline from Espire end to end: ingestion, chunking,
    embedding, storage, retrieval, reranking, prompt assembly, generation, citation.
15. How did you chunk? Fixed size, recursive, semantic, or structure-aware? What chunk
    size and overlap, and how did you arrive at those numbers rather than copying a blog?
16. How do you chunk a 200-page insurance policy PDF with tables and nested clauses?
    What does naive chunking destroy?
17. Which embedding model did you use and why? How would you decide between two of them
    for your domain?
18. pgVector vs ChromaDB vs Azure AI Search vs a dedicated vector DB - you've used
    several. Give me the real decision criteria, and where each falls over.
19. pgVector index types (IVFFlat vs HNSW): explain the recall/latency/build-time
    tradeoff and the parameters you'd tune.
20. What is hybrid search? Why does BM25 still beat embeddings for some queries?
    How do you combine them - and what is reciprocal rank fusion?
21. What is a reranker, where does it sit, and what does it cost you in latency?
    Did you use one? What improved?
22. Your RAG answers a question wrong. Walk me through the debugging tree: is it a
    retrieval failure, a chunking failure, a ranking failure, or a generation failure?
    How do you tell them apart?
23. How do you measure retrieval quality? (Looking for: recall@k, MRR, NDCG, and a
    labelled golden set.) Do you have a golden set? Who built it?
24. How do you measure answer quality? Faithfulness/groundedness, answer relevance,
    context precision/recall. Have you used RAGAS or an LLM-as-judge? What are the
    failure modes of LLM-as-judge?
25. How do you stop hallucination when retrieval returns nothing relevant? How does the
    system say "I don't know"?
26. How do you do citations so a user can verify the answer? What's hard about that?
27. Documents change. How do you handle incremental re-indexing, deletions, and stale
    embeddings? What happens when you switch embedding models - full re-embed?
28. Permissions in RAG: user A must not retrieve chunks from documents they can't see.
    How do you enforce that at the vector-search layer, not after?
29. Multi-tenant RAG: one index with a filter, or an index per tenant? Cost and
    isolation tradeoffs.
30. When is fine-tuning better than RAG, and when is neither the answer? What would
    you fine-tune for in the insurance domain?
31. What is GraphRAG / knowledge-graph RAG and when is it worth the complexity?
32. Query rewriting, HyDE, multi-query expansion - which have you tried and did they
    actually move a metric?

C. AGENTS, TOOL CALLING & MCP (he built both)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
33. What is function/tool calling, mechanically? What does the model actually emit?
34. What is MCP, what problem does it solve, and how is it different from just writing
    an API wrapper? Explain tools vs resources vs prompts.
35. Walk me through the MCP server you built for the Pulse QA team - what tools did it
    expose, and how did you handle auth and blast radius?
36. If an MCP tool can mutate production data, what guardrails do you build?
    (Expect: read-only defaults, scoping, confirmation, audit logging, sandboxing.)
37. How do you design a tool description so the model actually calls it correctly?
    What happens when you have 40 tools? How do you scale tool selection?
38. ReAct vs plan-and-execute vs a state-machine/graph workflow. Which did you use
    for the Cellular replacement agent and why?
39. Agent loops: how do you bound them? Max steps, budget caps, loop detection,
    and what do you do when the agent gets stuck repeating a call?
40. How do you handle a tool that fails or returns a huge payload that blows the
    context window?
41. Multi-agent systems - are they usually worth it, or is that mostly hype?
    Argue the honest position.
42. How do you make an agent's behaviour reproducible for debugging? What do you log
    per step? (Expect: full trace of prompts, tool calls, args, outputs, token counts.)
43. Your Cellular replacement agent: how do you prove to an auditor that the premium
    it computed is correct and repeatable? If you can't, why did you ship it?
44. Where should determinism live in an agentic system? (Looking for: LLM decides
    routing, deterministic code does the math.)
45. How would you evaluate an agent, as opposed to a single LLM call?
    (Trajectory eval, tool-selection accuracy, end-task success rate.)
46. What is human-in-the-loop approval design, and where did you need it?

51. Semantic caching - what is it, when does it help, and when is it dangerous?
52. How do you control and attribute LLM cost per tenant/feature? What do you track?
53. Self-hosted (Ollama/vLLM) vs API providers (Azure OpenAI, Groq): give me the real
    decision framework - cost curve, latency, data residency, ops burden.
54. What is vLLM doing that makes it fast? (PagedAttention, continuous batching -
    conceptually is enough.)
55. Groq is on your resume - what's the actual use case for that kind of latency, and
    did it change your product design?
56. How do you version prompts? Where do they live, how are they reviewed, and how do
    you roll back a bad prompt change?
57. How do you A/B test a prompt or model change in production safely?
58. What does observability look like for an LLM system? (Traces, token counts, cost,

59. A model provider deprecates the model version you depend on in 30 days.
    What is your plan, and what should you have built earlier?
60. Model outputs regress after a provider silently updates the model. How would you

62. PII and data governance: what leaves your network, what gets logged, what gets

63. Guardrails: input filtering, output validation, jailbreak detection, toxicity.
