=====================================================================
ROUND 4 - SYSTEM DESIGN (incl. Cloud & DevOps architecture)
Duration: 50 min assessment + 10 min general
=====================================================================

HOW TO RUN THIS ROUND
- Pick ONE main design (35-40 min) + 2-3 short "deep dive"/rapid questions (10-15 min).
- Do NOT pick more than one big design. Depth beats coverage here.
- Recommended pairings for this candidate:
    * Strongest signal:  Q21 (rating/quoting engine) or Q24 (document intake) - his domain
    * Fair + hard:       Q11 (news recommender) - he claims 100M/day, make him defend it
    * Neutral classic:   Q1 or Q3 - if you want an unfamiliar-domain baseline
    * DevOps-flavoured:  Q31 or Q33 - directly tests the ops half of the role
- Expect from a 7-year candidate: clarifying questions FIRST, explicit non-functional
  requirements, back-of-envelope numbers, a clean box diagram, then depth on 2 components,
  then failure modes and cost. If he starts drawing boxes in minute one, that's a flag.

SCORING RUBRIC (use per design)
  [ ] Clarified scope & asked about constraints before designing
  [ ] Stated NFRs: RPS, latency target, data volume, consistency, availability
  [ ] Did back-of-envelope math and it was roughly right
  [ ] API / data model before infrastructure
  [ ] Justified datastore choice with a reason, not a preference
  [ ] Identified the actual bottleneck unprompted
  [ ] Handled failure: retries, idempotency, partial failure, DLQ, degradation
  [ ] Talked about deployment, rollout, observability, and cost without being asked
  [ ] Named the tradeoffs he chose against, not just the ones he chose
  [ ] Knew what he'd build v1 vs what he'd defer

=====================================================================
PART 1 - CLASSIC / BLANKET DESIGNS (domain-neutral baseline)
=====================================================================
1.  Design a URL shortener. Then: 100k writes/sec, custom aliases, analytics, and
    link expiry. Where does it stop being trivial?
2.  Design a parking lot system. (Start OO/API design, then push to distributed:
    500 lots, real-time availability, reservations, and payments.)
3.  Design a rate limiter as a shared service used by 200 microservices.
4.  Design a distributed job scheduler (cron-as-a-service) with at-least-once
    execution, 1M jobs, and per-second granularity.
5.  Design a notification service: email, SMS, push, in-app. Templating, preferences,
    quiet hours, dedupe, and delivery guarantees.
6.  Design a file upload + processing service (S3/Blob, 5GB files, resumable,
    virus scan, transcode, webhook on complete).
7.  Design an idempotent payment/transaction ledger. Double-entry, no lost money,
    reconciliations.
8.  Design a feature flag service - low latency reads, global, with instant kill switch.
9.  Design a real-time collaborative dashboard (many readers, live updates, 50k
    concurrent connections).
10. Design an audit log service that is append-only, tamper-evident, queryable, and
    retains 7 years of data cheaply.

=====================================================================
PART 2 - DESIGNS DRAWN FROM HIS OWN RESUME (make him defend his claims)
=====================================================================
11. Design a news recommendation serving system for 100M requests/day.
    Push on: peak RPS, candidate generation vs ranking, precomputed vs online,
    cold start for new users AND new articles, feature store, cache hit ratio,
    freshness vs cost, and how you A/B test ranking changes.
12. Same system, now: the ML team wants to ship a new model twice a day, and you must
    support shadow traffic and instant rollback. Design the model serving layer.
13. Design an ATS resume-processing pipeline at 1M resumes/day: ingestion from many
    sources, parsing (PDF/DOC/scanned), dedupe of the same candidate across sources,
    embedding + matching against JDs, and ranking explainability.
14. Design a large-scale ecommerce crawler platform: 10k sites, daily, politeness,
    proxies/anti-bot, dynamic JS pages, schema drift detection, and a data-quality
    gate before customers see anything.
15. Design the Digital Shelf Analytics backend: multi-tenant, heavy analytical queries
    over billions of rows, sub-3s dashboards, daily ingestion. OLTP vs OLAP split,
    pre-aggregation strategy, and how you keep per-tenant cost visible.
16. Design a geospatial platform to replace ArcGIS (GeoStorm): store and query millions
    of features, spatial joins, tile serving, and SOC2-grade access control down to
    geometry level.
17. Design an RLHF annotation platform: task assignment, claim/expiry locks, streaming
    model outputs, multi-annotator agreement, quality scoring of annotators, and
    export to a training pipeline.
18. Design a serverless migration of a legacy monolithic SaaS. Strangler plan, what
    stays on VMs, cost model, and how you prove parity before cutting over.
19. Design a multi-region active-active deployment for a Python API + Postgres.
    What's genuinely hard? (Expect: write conflicts, data residency, session affinity,
    failover testing.) When is active-passive the correct, boring answer?
20. Design the infrastructure for a 10-person data science team so they can go from
    notebook to production endpoint without you. What do you own vs what do they own?

=====================================================================
PART 3 - INSURANCE / DOCUMENT AI DESIGNS (his current domain, highest signal)
=====================================================================
21. Design a dynamic rating & quoting engine. Requirements: business users author
    rating logic in Excel; 2,000 quotes/min; every quote must be reproducible years
    later; rules change weekly; multiple products and jurisdictions.
    Push on: versioning of rules, deterministic replay, caching, and rollout of a
    rule change without breaking in-flight quotes.
22. Extend it: rating rules must now be A/B tested, and an actuary must be able to see
    exactly which rule version produced a number. Design the lineage/audit model.
23. Design the quote -> bind -> issue policy workflow as a distributed transaction
    across 5 services, where one downstream (a carrier API) is slow and flaky.
24. Design an insurance submission intake system: agents email documents; extract
    structured data; route to underwriters; SLA of 15 minutes; must handle 200-page
    PDFs, scanned faxes, and duplicate submissions of the same risk.
25. Design the human-in-the-loop review layer for that extraction: how do you decide
    what needs review, how do you measure model quality over time, and how do you
    feed corrections back?
26. Design a policy document generation service: 100k documents/day at month end,
    templated, versioned, immutable once issued, retrievable for 10 years, and
    regionally compliant.
27. Design a system where an LLM agent replaces a deterministic rating engine, but
    the business needs deterministic, auditable, regulator-defensible outputs.
    Is this even the right architecture? Argue against your own resume.

=====================================================================
PART 4 - CLOUD & DEVOPS ARCHITECTURE (the ops half of this role)
=====================================================================
28. Design the full AWS (or Azure) landing zone for a new product: accounts/subscriptions,
    networking, identity, secrets, logging, cost allocation, and guardrails. Day 1 vs
    day 100.
29. Design CI/CD for 30 microservices in a monorepo. Selective builds, shared libs,
    versioning, environment promotion, and how long a deploy takes.
30. Design a zero-downtime deployment strategy for a service that holds long-lived
    connections and has a stateful in-memory cache.
31. We are on AKS, our cloud bill doubled in 3 months, and nobody knows why.
    Walk me through your investigation and the levers you'd pull, in order of
    effort vs saving.
32. Design a secrets management + rotation strategy across dev/stage/prod and 3 clouds.
    Include: what happens the day a prod DB credential leaks publicly.
33. Design the observability stack for a 40-service platform on a fixed budget.
    What do you sample, what do you keep, what do you drop, and what are the 6 alerts
    that page a human at 3am?
34. Design a disaster recovery plan for your most critical service. Define RTO/RPO,
    then tell me how you'd actually prove it works.
35. Design a self-service internal developer platform: a new service goes from
    "git repo created" to "running in prod with monitoring" with no platform-team
    ticket. What are the golden paths?
36. Design blue/green + canary for a service whose deploy includes a backward-
    incompatible DB migration. Sequence it precisely.
37. Design multi-tenant infrastructure isolation for an insurance SaaS with clients who
    demand data residency in EU, UK and US, and one client who demands a dedicated stack.
38. Design an autoscaling strategy for a workload with a 20x spike every day at 9am
    that lasts 20 minutes. Compare HPA, KEDA, scheduled scaling, and overprovisioning
    on cost.
39. Design the incident response process end to end - detection, paging, triage,
    comms, mitigation, postmortem. What is your definition of a blameless postmortem?
40. You inherit infrastructure with no IaC, 200 hand-built resources, and no one who
    remembers why. How do you get to Terraform without an outage?
41. Design a compliance-ready (SOC2) logging & access-review system: who accessed what
    customer data, when, retained for how long, and how you produce an auditor report.
42. Design the migration of a stateful Postgres workload from on-prem to cloud with
    under 5 minutes of downtime.

=====================================================================
PART 5 - RAPID DEEP-DIVE PROBES (10-15 min filler, use after main design)
=====================================================================
43. In your design, exactly where can data be lost? Name every point.
44. Where is the single point of failure you've accepted, and why is it acceptable?
45. Traffic goes 10x tomorrow with no warning. What breaks first, second, third?
46. Cut the cost of this design by 60%. What do you sacrifice?
47. Now build the same thing with a 2-person team in 6 weeks. What do you throw away?
48. Which component here would you buy instead of build, and what's the lock-in risk?
49. How do you migrate this design from v1 to v2 with live traffic and no downtime?
50. What would you monitor to know this system is healthy - give me the 5 dashboards
    and 5 alerts, no more.
51. Where in this design does eventual consistency become visible to a user, and is
    that acceptable?
52. If this system is down for 1 hour, what is the business impact, and does your
    design match that impact?
53. What part of your own design are you least confident about?
54. What did you deliberately leave out, and when would you add it?
